Worth fixing first

Your home page contains a hidden link to a gambling website.

Tucked into the code just under your main heading, and moved off the edge of the screen so visitors never see it, is a link to an online casino site. You almost certainly did not put it there.

Hidden links like this are usually added by someone who has got into a website without permission, to borrow its good name with Google. Search engines can mark a site down for it, and it does not belong on a family business's page.

It is worth asking whoever looks after your website to remove it, update the site and change its passwords. We have not touched anything on your site.

To fix

To fix

To fix

A new site would include those details, so your hours show up directly in search.

    Three places to play

    One frame for big climbers, one corner for little ones, and a sofa for you.

    The big frame

    Climbing wall, monkey bars, infinity mirror, rickety rope bridges, balance beams, lookout holes and the super wide slide.

    Under 3s

    A sensory area, a smaller slide, a ball pool, a swing seat and happy hoppers. Soft bricks and toys for crawlers.

    The café

    Freshly ground coffee, cakes and snacks, free wifi and comfy sofas. No charge for adults, so bring a friend.
